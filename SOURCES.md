# Sources — every story on the page

Checked 23 November 2026 against reporting of 21–22 November 2026. Where a
figure moved during the day, the page uses the figure reported on 22 November.

## Hero

- **COP31 ends with no deal (Antalya, Turkey).** The summit ended on Sunday
  after two days of overtime; the EU and about 130 parties rejected a draft
  with no mention of fossil fuels. Longest UN climate summit on record.
  Sources: BBC, One Turn (22 Nov edition), AP.

## Cards

1. **Warship hit at Benghazi port (Libya).** A suspected Ukrainian drone
   attack hit a Russian warship at Benghazi; a second ship was reported hit.
   Sources: One Turn (22 Nov edition), Standard Topic.
2. **Flats collapse in strike near Kyiv (Dymer, Ukraine).** A Russian strike
   partly collapsed an apartment block in Dymer, north of Kyiv; at least 3
   killed, a boy pulled alive from the rubble. Sources: AP, KIRO 7 / AP.
3. **Democrats win House by 258 (USA).** Democrats secured at least 258 US
   House seats, their largest majority since 1992, after a North Carolina
   recount. Source: One Turn (22 Nov edition).
4. **Iran strikes hit Bahrain site.** Iran struck a civilian industrial
   facility in Bahrain; two people hurt. Tankers were also hit near Hormuz.
   Sources: Reuters, AFP, Al Jazeera (21–22 Nov).
5. **El Chapo Isidro captured (Sinaloa, Mexico).** Special forces captured
   cartel boss Fausto Isidro Meza Flores on Saturday; 11 killed in the raid,
   including four soldiers. Sources: AP, Al Jazeera (21–22 Nov).
6. **FBI probes hack of crime files (USA).** The FBI is investigating an
   apparent hack of the NCIC crime database, first reported by CNN on Friday
   20 Nov and confirmed by AP on Saturday 21 Nov. Sources: AP, CNN,
   BleepingComputer.
